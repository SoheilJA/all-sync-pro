The stakes

Speed is revenue. Slow is a tax.

Every 100 milliseconds of latency measurably eats conversion — the studies agree even when the exact numbers vary. Meanwhile, the average agency build ships a 4-second LCP, a 4 MB JavaScript bundle nobody can explain, and a Lighthouse score that dips below 60 the moment the tracking scripts arrive.

We treat performance the way finance treats reconciliation: non-negotiable, measured, and owned by name. Budgets are agreed before the first commit, enforced on every pull request, and reported to you monthly — in plain English.

The compounding payoff is quieter than a fast first paint: code that new engineers can safely change on a Friday. That’s what “built to scale” actually means.

Service FAQ

Engineering questions, answered.

Contracts, ownership, and process live in the studio FAQ — or ask an engineer directly.

Do you really guarantee a Lighthouse score?
Yes — 95+ on Performance and 100 on Best Practices for Static and Platform builds, measured on the median field data three weeks post-launch (not a lab-only screenshot). If we miss the agreed budget for reasons within our control, we fix it on our time. Third-party scripts you add later can move the needle — we’ll flag that before it ships.
Which CMS do you recommend?
The boring answer: it depends on who edits the site. WordPress with a custom block kit for editorial teams; a headless setup (custom admin or Sanity-style) when the site is product-like; no CMS at all when content changes twice a year. We decide with your team in week one — and we’ve written custom admin panels that non-technical editors genuinely prefer.
Can you rescue our existing codebase?
Frequently — it’s roughly a quarter of our work. We start with a two-week audit (architecture, dependencies, security, performance), then propose either incremental hardening or a strangler-pattern replatform. We’ll tell you honestly when a rewrite is waste; staying on a working-but-ugly core is often the right economic call.
Who hosts the site?
You do, on your own accounts — Vercel, Netlify, AWS, or your preferred host — configured by us during launch week. We refuse vendor lock-in as policy: hosting, domains, DNS, and repositories all live under your organization from day one.
How do you handle security?
Baseline hardening on every build: dependency scanning in CI, CSP headers, least-privilege secrets, and OWASP-top-10 review on anything that touches auth or payments. Care plans add monthly patch cycles and an annual penetration test through a partner firm — because “secure” is a verb.
What happens if something breaks at 2 a.m.?
Monitoring pings us before it pings you — uptime, error rates, and Core Web Vitals from real users. Care-plan clients have a 48-hour fix SLA on P2 issues and a 4-hour acknowledgment on P1s, with a rehearsed rollback path so “incident” rarely means “outage.”
