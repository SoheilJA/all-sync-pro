The problem

Off-brand is a slow leak.

Brand equity rarely dies in one decision. It leaks — a Canva flyer here, a mismatched slide there, a “quick” social post an intern whipped up. Each one is individually forgivable. Together they teach your market that you are approximately who you claim to be.

The math is unforgiving: your audience meets your brand dozens of times before they ever meet your sales team. If those touchpoints argue for slightly different companies, the average of them is nobody. Graphic design, practiced as a system, is how you close the leak.

So we treat every deliverable as part of one argument. The deck, the brochure, the LinkedIn carousel, the packaging — same typographic voice, same color logic, same spine. Design that knows what it is defending.

How it runs

Five moves, three weeks to a full kit.

  1. 01

    Brief & audit

    Days 1–3

    We inventory every asset you ship and interrogate the argument: who it’s for, what it must prove, where it fails today. The audit alone usually pays for itself.

  2. 02

    Concept territories

    Days 4–8

    Three distinct visual directions, each fully art-directed on real deliverables — not mood boards. You pick the argument; we make it undeniable.

  3. 03

    Production

    Days 9–16

    The chosen territory rolls out across every asset in scope, reviewed live twice a week. Consistency is engineered here, not hoped for.

  4. 04

    Systemize

    Days 17–20

    Assets become templates with locked layers, named swatches, and a usage mini-guide. Your team ships on-brand without us in the room.

  5. 05

    Deliver & iterate

    Days 21+

    Source files, exports, and print specs handed over in one organized library — plus a revision window for whatever reality stress-tests first.

Service FAQ

Design questions, answered.

Contracts, file ownership, and timelines live in the studio FAQ — or just ask us directly.

Do you design for print or digital?
Both — and the difference matters more than people think. Print files are built in CMYK with bleed, safe margins, and stock notes your printer will thank you for; digital work ships in RGB at every required resolution and weight. Output targets are agreed up front, so nothing gets “converted” as an afterthought.
Do we own the source files?
Yes — fully, from final payment. You receive layered AI, PSD, and INDD files with fonts packaged or outlined, plus flattened exports in every format your channels need. Nothing is watermarked, rented, or held hostage for future revisions.
How fast can you turn around a rush job?
Standard production runs 12 days, but we hold two 48-hour rush slots per month for existing clients and emergencies. Rush work carries a 40% premium and still gets the same press-ready QA — speed never buys a shortcut past quality control.
How many concepts do we get to choose from?
Three territories in the Campaign Visual System, each art-directed on real deliverables so you judge outcomes, not vibes. We deliberately cap it there: ten directions means nobody has a point of view, and one means you’re trusting a stranger’s taste blind.
Can you work within our existing brand?
That’s most of our work. We audit your guidelines, adopt the type and color logic as constraints, and spend the creative budget where it pays — composition, hierarchy, craft. If a guideline is actively hurting comprehension, you’ll get evidence, not a designer’s opinion.
Do you use stock illustration or draw custom?
Custom by default — an illustration family drawn for your argument is an asset; stock is a rental everyone shares. When budget or timeline demands stock, we say so explicitly, license it properly, and customize it enough that it doesn’t read as a search result.
