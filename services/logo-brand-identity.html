What’s included

Not a logo. An operating system.

Everything a brand needs to be recognized, reproduced, and remembered — delivered as a system your whole organization can apply without calling us.

  • Logo suite

    Primary, secondary, monogram, and wordmark — each with defined use cases so the mark works at every size and context.

  • Construction & clear-space specs

    Geometry documented with measurements and exclusion zones — the mark protected from well-meaning meddling.

  • Color system

    A palette with HEX, RGB, CMYK, and Pantone references — plus accessibility-checked pairings for screen and print.

  • Launch collateral

The problem

A drawing vs. a system.

A $300 logo is a drawing. It looks fine on the invoice and dies on contact with reality — wrong colors on a partner’s slide, stretched on a storefront, recreated badly by a vendor who needed it yesterday. Not because the drawing was ugly, but because it arrived alone.

A real identity is a system that makes every future decision cheaper. Which variant for a favicon, what clear-space on a tradeshow banner, which color survives newsprint — answered in advance, in writing, with the files attached. The system pays rent every single day.

We engineer for the half-second test: your mark noticed and attributed before conscious thought kicks in. That is not decoration — that is compounding recognition, the cheapest advertising you will ever buy.

Service FAQ

Identity questions, answered.

Ownership terms and timelines live in the studio FAQ — or just ask us directly.

How many logo concepts do we get?
Three territories — genuinely different geometric and tonal approaches, each shown applied to signage, app icons, and merchandise. Widening beyond three just spreads doubt; deepening one is where the value lives. If none land, week two restarts at half cost — it has happened exactly twice.
Do you handle trademark registration?
We don’t — and you should be wary of designers who claim to. What we do: screen territories against obvious conflicts in your class and region, deliver everything your trademark attorney needs, and coordinate with counsel throughout. Legal clearance is theirs; certainty of craft is ours.
What files do we actually receive?
The full suite: vector masters in AI, EPS, and SVG; PNGs in every sensible size; favicon sets optimized for web; and print-ready one-color and reversed versions. All organized in a named, documented library your team can navigate without a designer in the room.
Can we keep our existing logo?
Often, yes — when there’s equity worth keeping. Logo Refresh exists precisely for marks that need geometry tightened, variations built, and a system wrapped around them. If the mark is beyond rescue, we’ll show you why with evidence, not taste.
Is naming included?
Only in Full Reposition, where naming exploration and screening support are part of the engagement. For Identity System projects we work happily with an already-named brand or coordinate with your naming consultant — the system is built to survive either starting point.
Who pays for font licenses?
You do — and the typography system tells you exactly which licenses, at which tiers, for how many seats, before you spend a cent. We recommend typefaces with clean commercial terms, provide free-licensed alternatives where budget demands, and package the specs so procurement becomes a formality.
