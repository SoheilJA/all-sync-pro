FAQ — the whole truth

Asked, answered.

Sixteen honest answers about working with us — including the ones most studios dodge. Anything missing? Ask directly.

01 / Working together

Process & people

How does a project actually start?
A 30-minute discovery call, a written proposal with fixed scope and price within 48 hours, and a kickoff workshop once signed. Between signature and kickoff: one week maximum — we hold production slots open for accepted proposals.
Who will I actually work with?
The people doing the work — a project lead (always a founder for new clients), a senior designer, and a senior engineer, plus specialists as needed. No account managers, no sales engineers, no “the team will pass this along.”
How do revisions and feedback work?
Structured rounds inside a shared workspace — comments pinned to actual screens, not long email threads. Starter includes two rounds; Growth and Custom are unlimited within the approved direction. Feedback windows are 48 hours on your side, which keeps momentum ours to maintain.
What if the project runs late?
If a delay is on our side, the timeline extends at our cost — full stop. If it originates from delayed feedback or scope changes on the client side, we re-baseline together with a new, honest date. In eight years we’ve missed two launch dates and comped both projects’ final payments.

02 / Scope & deliverables

What you receive

Who owns the files, code, and IP?
You do — fully, from final payment. Repositories, Figma files, fonts we licensed for you, deploy pipelines, documentation: transferred to your accounts, confirmed in writing. No lock-in, no license games, no “contact us for access.”
Do you write our content or use ours?
Both, comfortably. Growth and Custom include interview-based conversion copywriting. If you have writers, we run a messaging workshop and edit against the structure. AI-drafted blobs without editing are the one input we’ll push back on — your visitors can tell.
Can you work with our in-house team?
Roughly a third of our work is built or maintained by client teams. We deliver tokens and documentation your engineers will respect, join their standups during the build, and hold a design-QA window after launch. Our systems are designed to be adoptable — that’s the whole point.
What happens after launch — am I on my own?
Every project includes 30–60 days of post-launch care covering bugs, tweaks, and monitoring. After that: care plans from $290/month keep things healthy, and growth retainers keep them improving — but plenty of clients run happily on their own. Leaving well is part of building well.

03 / Money

Budgets & billing

How much should we budget?
A useful heuristic: 5–10% of what the site should earn or save annually. Landing sprints start at $3,900; full sites with brand systems run $8,500–$18,000; platforms start at $25,000. The pricing page breaks it down line by line.
What are the payment terms?
Projects above $10,000 split 40/40/20 — kickoff, design sign-off, launch. Smaller projects are 50/50. Monthly plans bill on the 1st. We invoice in USD, GBP, or EUR, accept cards and bank transfer, and never charge for quotes, calls, or honest “you’re not ready” advice.
Why not the cheapest quote we received?
Maybe it shouldn’t be! Cheap is right when you’re validating. But price the full cycle: the $3k site that converts at 1% costs more per customer than the $15k site converting at 4% — and the redo usually costs more than doing it properly once. We’ll happily tell you when cheap is the correct call for your stage.

04 / Fit

Are we right for each other?

What clients do you work best with?
Founders and teams who treat their website as a revenue asset, not a brochure: SaaS, e-commerce, fintech, hospitality, and professional services. The pattern in our best work is clients who answer hard questions fast and let evidence settle debates.
When are you the wrong studio for us?
Honest list: you need a cheap template installed; you want hourly micromanagement; a committee of nine must approve every pixel; the goal is “make it pop” with no metric attached; or you need native mobile apps (we partner with two excellent studios and will introduce you).
Do you have capacity right now?
We keep a waiting list of 4–6 weeks rather than overbooking — it’s how every project still gets founders’ attention. Ask on the contact page; we’ll give you the real date, not a hopeful one.
