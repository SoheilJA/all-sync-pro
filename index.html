03 / Process

Five steps. Zero dropped threads.

01 / 05

  1. STEP 01 Week 0–1

    Discover

    We interrogate the business, not the brief. Stakeholder interviews, analytics forensics, and competitor teardown until we understand what “working” means for you.

    • Stakeholder interviews & KPI mapping
    • Analytics & funnel forensics
    • Technical audit of the current stack
  2. STEP 02 Week 1–2

    Strategy

    Positioning, messaging architecture, and a measurable conversion plan. Every later decision gets judged against this document — including ours.

    • Positioning & messaging architecture
    • Sitemap, flows & conversion plan
    • Success metrics we sign together
  3. STEP 03 Week 2–5

    Design

    Art direction first, then interface at full fidelity — tokens, components, and motion specs delivered as a system, not a pile of screens.

    • Art direction & hi-fi UI
    • Design system & motion spec
    • Interactive prototype for testing
  4. STEP 04 Week 4–8

    Build

    Engineered against a hard performance budget with semantic markup, accessibility baked in, and a staging link you can refresh every single day.

    • Semantic, accessible front-end build
    • CMS / commerce integration
    • QA on real devices, weekly demos
  5. STEP 05 Week 8+

    Launch & Scale

    Zero-downtime launch, analytics wired, team trained. Then we stay: iterating on real data, shipping improvements every month.

    • Launch, monitoring & handover docs
    • Team training & support SLA
    • Monthly experiments that compound

11 / FAQ

Asked, answered.

The questions every founder asks before the first call — answered honestly, including the uncomfortable ones.

Full FAQ
How much does a website from AllSyncPro cost?
Landing-page sprints start at $3,900. Full websites with a brand system typically land between $8,500 and $18,000. Platforms and web apps are scoped individually from $25,000. Every quote is fixed-price against a signed scope — the number you approve is the number you pay, unless you change the scope in writing.
How long does a typical project take?
A landing sprint runs 2–3 weeks. A full site ships in 4–6 weeks from kickoff to launch. Larger builds run 8–12 weeks. We publish the schedule before we start and demo progress weekly — you will never wonder where your project is.
Do you work with early-stage startups?
Constantly — about a third of our clients are pre-Series A. The Starter sprint exists precisely for that stage: enough craft to look funded, enough restraint to preserve runway. We also offer growth-friendly payment splits on projects above $10,000.
Who owns the design files and code?
You do — completely, from final payment. You receive the Figma files, the repository, and the deploy pipeline. No escrow, no lock-in, no “license” nonsense. If you leave, you take everything with you and it keeps working.
Do you write the website copy?
Yes — conversion copywriting is included in every full project. We interview your team and your customers, then write messaging that sells outcomes instead of adjectives. If you have an in-house writer, we collaborate instead of steamrolling.
What happens after launch?
Every project includes 30 days of post-launch care. After that, most clients move to a care plan from $290/month covering updates, monitoring, and small improvements — or a growth retainer where we keep shipping conversion experiments.
Which industries do you know well?
SaaS, e-commerce, fintech, hospitality, and professional services are our home turf — roughly 80% of shipped work. We deliberately stay narrow: deep industry pattern knowledge is why our first drafts already feel considered.
How do we communicate during the project?
A shared Slack channel with the actual makers (no account-manager relay), a weekly 30-minute demo call, and a live staging link from week one. You’ll watch the site grow in real time — most clients say it feels like their team got bigger.
